About LifeEasyChofu

A smart living guide designed to help international students and residents enjoy a comfortable life in Chofu City.

Our Mission

Moving to a new city can be challenging, especially when information is difficult to find. LifeEasyChofu collects important daily life information in one simple platform.

Our mission is to make Chofu living easier by providing clear guidance about garbage disposal, shopping places and useful local locations.

What We Offer

Garbage Guide

Learn garbage separation rules, collection schedules and disposal methods in an easy way.

Shopping Guide

Discover supermarkets, restaurants, clothing stores and affordable shops around Chofu.

Interactive Map

Explore useful places near UEC campus and Chofu City through an interactive map.

Who We Are

LifeEasyChofu is a student group project created to support people who are starting their life in Chofu City.

By sharing practical information and local knowledge, we hope to reduce confusion and help newcomers feel comfortable in their new community.
